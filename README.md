## Abstract
Perturbations by the 2022 Hunga-Tonga Volcano Eruption in the MLT Region Investigated Using the WACCM-X Simulation and Meteor Radar Observations

The High-resolution Whole Atmosphere Community Climate Model with thermosphere/ionosphere extension (WACCM-X) and meteor radars at different latitudes are utilized to simulate and observe the atmospheric responses to the eruption of the Hunga-Tonga Volcano on January 15, 2022. Specifically, the meteor radars located at Mohe (53.5°N, 122.3°E), Beijing (40.3°N, 116.2°E), Mengcheng (33.3°N, 116.5°E), Wuhan (30.5°N, 114.2°E), and Sanya (18.3°N, 109.6°E) are used. The simulation results are compared to the ground-based meteor radar observations regarding perturbations of wind, neutral density, and temperature in the Mesosphere and Lower Thermosphere (MLT) region. By combining simulations and observations, we aim to elucidate the dynamic mechanism underlying the MLT region's atmospheric perturbations triggered by the eruption of the Hunga-Tonga Volcano Eruption.
